## Features
- 10th and 1st Year Aggregate Calculator
  - With Mock NET Score included
- The Required MCQs needed to pass the aggregate of 79% for SEECS based on SSC/HSSC marks
- Log every day's Mock NET Scores with Time Taken to complete it

## Working
Asks you for your 10th and 1st Year marks and Mock NET marks and time taken to complete it. It then tells you the aggregate you have achieved right now with your 10th and 1st year marks, and a total aggregate including your Mock NET Marks. It also logs today's date and your Mock NET marks and the Time Taken to complete it to a CSV file created when you log for the first time.

## Why did I create NET Merit Analyzer?
At the time of creating this program, I am a 2nd Year Student preparing for the NETs. But the problem I fall into the most is that I have to manually calculate my scores and log my records everyday which is time consuming and frankly a little infuriating.
So I searched for something online and all I found was trash. Therefore, I decided to create it myself for everyone to use!
The program is still in the making and I hope others will find it useful.
